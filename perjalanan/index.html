Every unit is a chapter.

In Kancil, your child travels through 19 stops, from the jungle school to the robot workshop. Each stop opens with a short story, teaches the words, and ends with a crocodile to outsmart. Five minutes a day, in Malay.

How every unit plays.

The same four beats, so kids always know what comes next, and always want to see what's across the river.

1

ArriveCerita

A three-panel story sets the scene: Kancil arrives somewhere new.

2

Three words to findCari perkataan

Tap to hear each one in native Malay, then spot them in the lessons.

3

Learn on the trailBelajar

Short lessons: pictures, sentences, imbuhan. Longer units have a treasure stop halfway.

4

Cross the riverSungai Buaya

Every unit ends with a boss. Right answers are hops across the crocodiles' backs.

The crocodiles remember.

Sang Ketua and his crocs are recurring rivals. The first time, your child learns Kancil's famous counting trick. After that, the crocs come back grumpier, and the chief keeps count of every time he's been fooled, out loud, in Malay.

Beat the boss and the treasure chest opens. Then the next stop unlocks.
