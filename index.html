A little Malay. A new word today.

Kancil is a Malay learning game for kids from Primary 1 to PSLE, in Singapore, Malaysia and Brunei. Five minutes a day, with spoken Malay audio, a comic-book story and a crocodile boss to beat.

Follow the trail through every unit.

Each unit is a short walk through the hutan: learn the words, hear the story, beat the boss, open the chest.

Belajar

Learn by picture and sound

Every word comes with a picture and spoken Malay audio. Younger children start with Dengar & Pilih: listen, then tap the picture, no reading needed. English help is there when your child needs it, and quietly steps back when they don't.

Cerita

A story to step into

From Tahap 2 up, every unit opens with a page from Kancil's Jurnal: a short scene and three new words to find. Beat the unit's boss to unlock the next page of Buku Cerita: Hikayat Sang Kancil, a comic of the classic tales starring the Kancil your child plays.

Sungai Buaya

Battle the boss

Every unit ends at Sungai Buaya. Complete the sentence, build the ayat, choose the right imbuhan: every right answer is a hop across the crocodiles' river.

Harta

Open the treasure

Cross the river and a chapter chest opens. New words go into the Buku Kata sticker album, and every Tahap ends with a finale.

Screen time, earned in Malay. On your terms.

Kancil keeps a simple ledger between you and your child. They earn coins by learning. You decide what the coins are worth, and you grant the minutes.

Set the exchange rate in Untuk Ibu Bapa, the grown-up page. Coins turn into minutes only when you approve them, behind your PIN. Kancil never locks or controls the device.

Built on the syllabus

Four Tahap: Primary 1–2 (new, listen-first), Primary 3–4, Primary 5, and Primary 6 · PSLE. Mapped to the MOE Primary Malay Language Syllabus (2024), with Tahun and Darjah equivalents for Malaysia and Brunei. Independent app, not affiliated with MOE.

Private by design

No ads, no account, no ad tracking. Your child's progress is stored on your device. Anonymous usage stats help us improve lessons: no names, and you can switch them off.

Pay once, keep forever

The first unit of every Tahap is free forever. Unlock a Tahap with one payment, no subscription.

Made for families

A profile and a forest friend for each child, a daily reminder at a time you pick, and a parent area in English or Bahasa Melayu.
