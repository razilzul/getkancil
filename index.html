Why parents choose Kancil

Duolingo won't teach your child Malay. Kancil will.

A real game, not flashcards

Word–picture matching, susun ayat, imbuhan drills, peribahasa cards and native-quality Malay audio — with streaks, coins and a mascot that celebrates every win.

🪙

Learning earns screen time

Lessons earn coins; you decide what they're worth. Kancil never controls the device — it's a trust ledger between you and your child, behind your PIN.

📚

Built on the syllabus

130+ lessons across three skill levels. In Singapore, aligned to the MOE Bahasa Melayu 2024 syllabus up to the PSLE band; mapped to Tahun 1–6 for Malaysia & Brunei.

🔒

Private by design

No ads. No account. No analytics. No tracking. Your child's progress never leaves the device — there's no server for it to go to.

The screen-time contract

The #1 parent–kid fight, turned into the reward loop.
