Follow the trail through every unit.

Each unit is a short walk through the hutan: learn the words, hear the story, beat the boss, open the chest.

Learn by picture and soundBelajar

Every word comes with a picture and native Malay audio. English help is there when your child needs it, and quietly steps back when they don't.

A story to step intoCerita

Sang Kancil needs to reach school, and the river is full of crocodiles. Each unit opens with a short scene from the folktale.

Battle the bossLawan Sang Ketua

Complete the sentence, build the ayat, choose the right imbuhan. Every right answer is a hop across Sang Buaya's river.

Screen time, earned in Malay. On your terms.

Kancil keeps a simple ledger between you and your child. They earn coins by learning. You decide what the coins are worth, and you grant the minutes.

Built on the syllabus

Tahap 1–3 with a short placement quiz. Mapped to the MOE Primary Malay Language Syllabus (2024), with Tahun and Darjah equivalents shown for Malaysia and Brunei. Independent, not affiliated with MOE.

Private by design

No ads, no account, no tracking. Your child's progress is stored on your device.
