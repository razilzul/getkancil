Kancil keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts to log into, so most fixes happen right inside the app. Tap the gear button at the top of the home screen: Parents Dashboard (progress, coins, screen time) asks for your PIN, and Parent Settings opens straight away, with a PIN check on the rows that need one.
What's free, and what does it cost?

Kancil has four levels (Tahap). The first unit of every Tahap is free to play and replay forever. After that, you unlock a Tahap with a single one-time payment, with no subscription and nothing that renews. Each Tahap is S$6.98 at the launch price until 31 December 2026, then S$14.98. New families are also offered the Semua 4 Tahap bundle in the app, all four for less than the price of three. Families who bought a Tahap before 1 October 2026 keep "buy any three, get the fourth free". The app shows prices in your local currency before you confirm.

Which Tahap fits my child?

Tahap 1 is Primary 1–2 (listen-first, no reading needed). Tahap 2 is Primary 3–4, Tahap 3 is Primary 5, and Tahap 4 is Primary 6 and PSLE. The app also shows the Tahun and Darjah equivalents for Malaysia and Brunei. Your child can move between Tahap on the home map at any time, so if a level feels too easy or too hard, just switch.

Why did my child's Tahap number change?

We added a new Tahap 1 for Primary 1–2, so the older levels moved up one number: the old Tahap 1, 2 and 3 are now Tahap 2, 3 and 4. The lessons inside them haven't changed, and nothing is lost: progress, stars, coins, screen-time settings and purchases all carry over.

I was subscribed before. What happens now?

Thank you for backing Kancil early! Families who subscribed before we switched to one-time purchases keep every level, permanently, at no extra cost. Your old subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it — on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling does not remove your access. If you later move to a new phone and access doesn't come back, email us and we'll send a free unlock code.

I already paid — how do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Kancil, reach the unlock screen, and tap "Restore purchase." Make sure you're signed in to the Google or Apple account that bought or redeemed the level. On iPhone, eligible purchases can also restore through Family Sharing when purchase sharing is enabled.

I forgot the parent PIN. What now?

On the PIN screen, tap "Forgot PIN?". Kancil asks you to multiply two 2-digit numbers (a grown-up can use a calculator). Get it right and you choose a new 4-digit PIN. Your child's progress, coins and purchases stay as they are. We can't look up or reset the PIN for you: the PIN never leaves the phone.

How do I reset progress or start over?

Tap the gear on the home screen, then Parent Settings → Advanced → "Reset Kancil". It erases every child's progress, coins and history on this phone, and asks you to confirm first. This can't be undone. Your purchases are kept, and "Restore purchases" brings them back on any phone. Deleting the app also erases all progress. To remove just one child, use the Child section in Parent Settings instead.

Can brothers and sisters each have their own profile?

Yes. In Parent Settings → Child, tap "Add a child". Each child gets their own name, mascot, coins, streak and progress. The parent PIN and any purchases are shared, so you only pay once for the family's phone.

How does the daily reminder work?

Kancil can nudge your child at a time you pick (between 4 pm and 8 pm) on days they haven't played yet. After a few missed days it reminds every other day, and it stops after two weeks without play. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself: there is no push server and nothing is sent to us. Switch it off or change the time in Parent Settings → Reminders.

Can I use the parent screens in Bahasa Melayu?

Yes. The parent area (PIN screen, dashboard, settings and the buy screen) can be shown in English or Bahasa Melayu. Change it in Parent Settings → Language. Your child's lessons don't change.

What is Buku Cerita?

Buku Cerita is Hikayat Sang Kancil, a comic of the classic Sang Kancil tales starring the Kancil your child plays, told in Malay with English help. Each boss your child beats, and the halfway point of the longer units, unlocks the next page. Part 1 (pages 1 to 8) is written; later pages arrive with app updates and show as coming soon until then. You can read Part 1 free on this site.

How do I switch off the anonymous usage stats?

Parent Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage stats". When it's off, nothing is sent from that phone. The stats never include your child's name, mascot, PIN or anything typed. Our Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent.
