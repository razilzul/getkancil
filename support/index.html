Kancil keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts to log into — so most fixes happen right inside the app's parent area (tap the 🔒 button on the home screen and enter your PIN).
What's free, and what does it cost?

The first unit in every level is free to play and replay forever. After that, you unlock a level (Tahap) with a single one-time payment — no subscription, nothing renews. Buy any two levels and the third unlocks free, or get all three at once for the price of two. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app.

I was subscribed before. What happens now?

Thank you for backing Kancil early! Families who subscribed before we switched to one-time purchases keep every level, permanently, at no extra cost. Your old subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it — on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling does not remove your access. If you later move to a new phone and access doesn't come back, email us and we'll send a free unlock code.

I already paid — how do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Kancil, reach the unlock screen, and tap "Restore purchase." Make sure you're signed in to the Google or Apple account that bought or redeemed the level. On iPhone, eligible purchases can also restore through Family Sharing when purchase sharing is enabled.

I forgot the parent PIN. What now?

The PIN is stored only on your device and we can't look it up or reset it remotely (that's the point — nothing leaves the phone). If you're locked out, the "Reset to scratch" flow on a fresh reinstall clears local data including the PIN. Note this also clears your child's progress, since progress lives on the device only. Email us if you get stuck.

How do I reset progress or start over?

In the parent area (🔒 on the home screen) there's a "Reset to scratch" control that wipes all progress, coins, streak, screen-time history and the play log on that device — back to a fresh install. This can't be undone. Deleting the app does the same thing.
